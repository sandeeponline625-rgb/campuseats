\# CampusEats Brief

\## What

CampusEats is a web-based platform that allows students and staff to order food from campus vendors and have it delivered conveniently.

\## Who

\- Students who want quick access to meals

\- Campus staff who prefer delivery to offices

\- Food vendors who list their menus and fulfill orders

\- Delivery staff who handle logistics

\## Nouns

\- Students

\- Vendors

\- Menus

\- Orders

\- Payments

\- Delivery staff

\## Verbs

\- Browse menus

\- Place orders

\- Accept orders

\- Deliver food

\- Make payments

\- Track deliveries
